COMP396 TRADING RULES — BT396 0.1.5

Decide using information available by the close of day k. Orders are eligible
on the following trading bar, day k+1. Do not use future prices for signals.

For each series and decision day you may submit:
  - one market order, with a signed quantity;
  - two independent limit orders, each with a signed quantity and limit price.
The limits may both be buys, both sells, or one of each. Strategy components
share these allowances. Zero quantities do not submit orders.

Market orders execute at the next open. The additional cash cost is:
  s_mult * 0.2 * abs(open[k+1] - close[k]) * abs(quantity)
The standard s_mult is 1.0. This cost applies to markets, including mandatory
closing trades, and not to limits.

Limit execution preserves the R v5.9 course model. On the eligible bar:
  BUY:  low < limit - 0.0001; fill at min(limit, high).
  SELL: high > limit + 0.0001; fill at max(limit, low).
An exact touch does not fill. Native Backtrader opening-price improvement is
not used. No volume partial-fill model is applied. Unfilled limits are cancelled
after their single eligible bar and do not carry to another day.

The broker calculates all eligible ordinary market and limit fills across
all series together, including execution costs and configured commissions.
If total net spending exceeds cash, none of those ordinary trades execute.
Existing positions remain. Sale proceeds can fund purchases in that basket;
submission order does not determine whether a funded basket fills.
There is no separate penalty for an overspending attempt.

overspend_guard(intents) is an optional estimate using known closing prices
and commission. It cannot know tomorrow's gap or eligible future limit fills
and does not guarantee acceptance. The broker makes the definitive check on
the execution bar. A target adjustment is target minus current position.

Shorting is allowed. Account value is cash plus signed holdings. Bankruptcy
is checked at the open, including after execution costs. Positions are closed
at that open with market costs and subsequent strategy trading stops.
Mandatory bankruptcy/final exits are outside ordinary submission allowances
and affordability rejection. Corrected liquidation/accounting is retained.

The standard end policy is liquidate: each feed closes at its own final open,
with market slippage, and may not be reopened at that boundary. The hold option
is for experiments; use the assessment's prescribed setting.

Only independent Market and Limit orders are supported. Native stops, OCO,
brackets and volume fillers are not part of this course model. Implement a
daily stop-loss decision with an allowed market exit; the next open may be
beyond the chosen threshold.

METRICS

PD uses daily closing net P/L, including execution costs. Positive final profit
is divided by maximum monetary drawdown, with an initial zero baseline. Positive
profit with no drawdown gives infinity; non-positive final outcomes give signed
net P/L. Portfolio PD uses the combined curve, not an average of series scores.

Activity is the percentage of observed daily bars with a nonzero net position
in at least one series, including flat boundary bars in the denominator.
These conventions are retained deliberately rather than restoring the R
open-based curve and N-2 denominator.
